What I do

Four ways I get a site to standard

From a first read of where you stand to the documentation your procurement and legal teams ask for.

Audit

Accessibility audits

A full WCAG 2.2 and Section 508 review, with every issue ranked by severity and mapped to the exact success criterion — so your team knows what to fix first, not just that something's wrong.

Fix

Remediation

Hands-on fixes for what the audit finds: code, content, color, structure, and PDF remediation in Acrobat — plus accessible templates your team can reuse without me.

Document

VPAT & conformance reports

A VPAT 2.x / Accessibility Conformance Report that documents how your product meets the standard — the exact document a government buyer requires before they sign.

Maintain

Ongoing monitoring

Regular checks so new pages and content don't quietly slip out of compliance. Set up in Siteimprove or the tool your team already runs.

Who it's for

Built for the organizations that have to get this right

Deadline in effect

State & local government

The updated ADA Title II rule sets firm deadlines for web and mobile accessibility. I help you meet them — before a complaint or a deadline does it for you.

Health centers & FQHCs

Patient portals, scheduling tools, and public sites have to work for everyone, including people using screen readers and keyboards. I make sure they do.

Vendors selling to government

If your buyer requires a VPAT or Section 508 conformance, I produce the documentation and the fixes that clear the requirement and close the deal.
